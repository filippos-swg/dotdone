Dot It — Support

Last updated: October 2026

Dot It (formerly DotDone) is a minimal habit tracker. Tap to log a dot. That's it.

How it works

Tap anywhere on the home screen to record a dot for today. Hold to choose a task and assign a color to your dot. All your dots are stored locally on your device — nothing is sent anywhere.

Tasks

Tap MY TASKS in the footer to create, rename, reorder, or delete tasks. Each task gets a unique color. You can have up to 36 tasks. To change a task, tap its name in the list.

Calendar

Tap CALENDAR to see your dots by day. Use the arrows to move between days or weeks. Switch to monthly view using the toggle at the bottom. Long press any dot in the list to delete it.

Reminders

Open a task in MY TASKS to turn on a daily reminder and choose its time. Notification permission is requested when you enable it. Logging a dot in the app before the reminder time silences that day's reminder.

Export

Tap EXPORT DATA at the bottom of MY TASKS to save or share a JSON copy of your tasks and dots. Keep the file somewhere safe if you want your own backup. The app currently does not import exported files.

Home-screen widget

Open Dot It once after updating, then add the Dot It widget from your iPhone's widget gallery. On iOS 17 or later, edit the widget to choose a task and tap its dot to record without opening the app. On older supported iOS versions, tapping the widget opens Dot It so you can record there.

Deleting data

To delete a single dot: go to Calendar, select the day, long press the dot. To delete a task: go to My Tasks, tap the task name, tap DELETE.
