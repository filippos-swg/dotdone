DotDone — Support

Last updated: May 2026

DotDone is a minimal habit tracker. Tap to log a dot. That's it.

How it works

Tap anywhere on the home screen to record a dot for today. Hold to choose a task and assign a color to your dot. All your dots are stored locally on your device — nothing is sent anywhere.

Tasks

Tap MY TASKS in the footer to create, rename, reorder, or delete tasks. Each task gets a unique color. You can have up to 36 tasks. To change a task, tap its name in the list.

Calendar

Tap CALENDAR to see your dots by day. Use the arrows to move between days or weeks. Switch to monthly view using the toggle at the bottom. Long press any dot in the list to delete it.

Deleting data

To delete a single dot: go to Calendar, select the day, long press the dot. To delete a task: go to My Tasks, tap the task name, tap DELETE.
