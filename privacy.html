Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Dot It (formerly DotDone) is a simple habit tracking app. This policy explains what data we collect — and more importantly, what we don't.

Data we collect

None. Dot It does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Data stored on your device

Dot It stores your tasks and dot entries (including the date and time of each tap) locally on your device. If you choose Export Data, you control where the exported file goes through the iOS share sheet. We do not receive it or back it up to our servers.

Analytics and tracking

Dot It contains no analytics, no tracking SDKs, and no advertising.

Account

Dot It requires no account and does not ask for any personal information.

Children

Dot It does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date.
