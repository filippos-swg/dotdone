Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

DotDone is a simple habit tracking app. This policy explains what data we collect — and more importantly, what we don't.

Data we collect

None. DotDone does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Data stored on your device

DotDone stores your dot entries (date and time of each tap) locally on your device using the device's file system. This data never leaves your device. It is not backed up to any server controlled by us.

Analytics and tracking

DotDone contains no analytics, no tracking SDKs, no advertising, and no third-party services of any kind.

Account

DotDone requires no account and does not ask for any personal information.

Children

DotDone does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date.
